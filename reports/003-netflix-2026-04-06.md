## Block C — Level & Strategy

### Level Positioning

Netflix "Manager, Engineering" = first-line people manager. At Netflix, this is NOT a tech lead or staff IC role. Key expectations:

1. **Hire, coach, develop, retain** a team of senior/staff engineers
2. **Set technical vision** and roadmap (but not necessarily write code daily)
3. **Partner with product** to build iterative roadmaps
4. **Drive execution** -- timely delivery, not just architecture

Patrick's current trajectory is **Staff IC**. The transition to EM requires framing:

### Positioning Strategy

**Lead with:** "I've been the technical architect who built the observability platform my teams use. Now I want to be the leader who builds the team that builds the platform."

**Frame the Watchtower work as proto-management:**
- Led team of 4 engineers
- Drove adoption across 40+ teams (influencing without authority)
- Set technical vision (DORA metrics, Four Golden Signals framework)
- Operated as the connective tissue between product teams and platform

**Acknowledge the transition honestly:** Netflix values candor. Don't pretend to have been an EM. Instead: "I've been the technical leader my team looked to for direction. I've realized the highest-leverage thing I can do is multiply that across a team I build and grow."

### Level Fit: Stretch

This is a meaningful stretch from Staff IC to first EM role. Netflix typically hires experienced managers, not first-time managers, especially for a newly formed team. The "build a team from scratch" mandate usually goes to proven builders.

## Block D — Comp & Demand

### Compensation Analysis

Netflix's unique comp structure: **all cash, no bonuses, no RSU vesting schedule**. Employees choose annually how much to take as salary vs. stock options.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Netflix EM (Warsaw, Manager level)** | PLN 536K-793K/year (~$130K-$195K USD) |
| **Netflix EM (Warsaw, median)** | PLN 718K/year (~$177K USD) |
| **Netflix EM (US, Manager level)** | $500K-$800K/year |
| **Patrick's target** | $200K-$230K USD |

**Key insight:** The JD does not disclose a salary range (text says "The range for this role is ." -- blank). Warsaw comp is significantly below US Netflix comp. At the top of the Warsaw range (~$195K USD), it barely touches Patrick's minimum ($185K). At median ($177K), it's below minimum.

However, Netflix Poland comp is still elite for the Polish market. Cost of living in Warsaw is roughly 55-60% of Denver.

### Market Demand

Observability engineering management is a **high-demand, low-supply** specialty. Few candidates combine:
- Observability domain expertise
- People management experience
- Willingness to work on-site in Warsaw

This scarcity works in Patrick's favor IF he's willing to relocate.

### Comp Score: 2.5/5

Below target range in absolute USD terms. The all-cash structure is attractive but Warsaw location means a significant pay cut vs. US roles. Would only make sense if relocation to Europe is a life goal, not just a career move.

### Score Breakdown

The technical match is genuinely strong -- Patrick has built almost exactly this kind of platform. But three factors pull the score down significantly:

1. **Location (critical):** On-site Warsaw, Poland. Patrick is in Denver, CO. This requires international relocation, work authorization in Poland/EU, and uprooting life. This alone would normally make this a SKIP.

If relocation IS on the table: this would be a compelling stretch opportunity. The Watchtower story maps almost perfectly to the role. Patrick should network with Netflix Observability engineers to gauge whether first-time EM candidates are considered.
