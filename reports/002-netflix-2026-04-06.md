## F) Interview Plan

### Netflix Culture Memo Emphasis

Netflix interviews heavily weight culture fit. Key principles to internalize:
- **Freedom & Responsibility** -- high autonomy, high accountability
- **Context, not control** -- set direction, trust the team
- **People over process** -- hire great people, minimize rules
- **Disagree then commit** -- healthy debate, unified execution
- **Keeper test** -- "would I fight to keep this person?"
