## C) Level and Strategy

### Level Analysis

**JD Level:** Mid-Senior IC (3+ yr requirement, $140K-$265K base). No "Staff" or "Senior" prefix in title -- just "Software Engineer." Likely maps to Glean's L3-L5 equivalent.

**Patrick's natural level:** Staff (10+ yr experience, led teams of 4, architected platforms for 100M+ users). Patrick would likely be placed at the senior end of the band or above.

### Strategy: Sell Senior/Staff without Overshoot

- Frame as: "Staff-level engineer who wants to go deep on a hard technical problem rather than manage." The Data Foundations team is building critical infrastructure -- this is where senior ICs have outsized impact.
- Lead with: Petabyte-scale experience (federal platforms, 100M+ users), distributed systems correctness (idempotent pipelines, zero-downtime migrations), and production observability culture.
- Position Rust/Go proficiency as a strength: "I optimize for correctness and performance at the systems level -- that's why I moved from Python to Rust and Go for backend work."

### Strategy: If Down-leveled

- The $140K-$265K base range is wide. If placed at mid-band ($200K), total comp with equity at a $7.2B unicorn could be compelling.
- Negotiate: "Happy to start at L[X] with a 6-month review and clear promotion criteria. My track record speaks for itself."
- Equity at Glean's growth stage (pre-IPO, $7.2B, $100M+ ARR) could be significant upside.
