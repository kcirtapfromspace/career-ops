### What this role actually is

This is a **people-manager-first** role with strong technical expectations. The person will:

1. **Hire, coach, grow** a high-performing data engineering team
2. **Own the roadmap** for commerce data products (sign-up, payment, acquisition)
3. **Build shared tooling** -- reusable frameworks, dev tooling, data modeling standards
4. **Partner with central Data Platform** as primary technical liaison
5. **Enable experimentation** -- the data products feed Product and DS for A/B tests

The commerce domain is a revenue-critical function at Netflix. This is not a backwater team -- it directly impacts how Netflix acquires and retains paying members globally.

## C) Level and Strategy

### Level Analysis

| Dimension | JD Level | Patrick's Level |
|-----------|----------|----------------|
| **Title** | Manager | No formal Manager title (Staff Architect, Senior Engineer) |
| **People management** | 5+ years managing DE teams | ~3 years leading small teams (4 engineers max), no direct reports in org-chart sense |
| **Technical depth** | Strong DE + distributed systems | Staff-level DE, exceeds technical bar |
| **Scope** | Team-level roadmap ownership | Project-level ownership (Watchtower, Colorado Payroll, Argo platform) |
| **Stakeholders** | Cross-functional (Product, DS, Eng, Finance) | Cross-functional (40+ teams, multi-agency) |

**Assessment:** Patrick's technical depth exceeds the bar. The gap is entirely on the people management side. Netflix is explicit about "5+ years engineering leadership managing Data Engineering teams." Patrick has roughly 3 years of team leadership with a 4-person team being the largest. This is the make-or-break dimension.

### Strategy: "Sell Manager without lying"

1. **Lead with scale of impact, not headcount.** "Architected and delivered data platforms serving 100M+ users across federal agencies. Led a team of engineers through delivery of the VA's standard observability platform, defining roadmap, hiring, and coaching."
2. **Frame Ad Hoc role as de facto EM.** "As Staff Data Architect, I owned hiring, roadmap, architecture decisions, and delivery for my team. I built the team from scratch, coached engineers, and aligned priorities across 40+ product teams."
3. **Workiva as team-building context.** "Led adoption of Kubernetes-native orchestration and Data Mesh architecture across the Data Management & Analytics organization, enabling 200+ analysts."
4. **Use Netflix's own culture language.** Netflix values "judgment" and "context, not control." Frame: "I lead by setting clear context and trusting talented engineers to execute. My teams shipped because they understood the why, not because I micromanaged the how."

### Plan: "If they downlevel"

Netflix doesn't traditionally downlevel -- they either hire at the role or don't. However, if they suggest a Senior Engineer (IC) role instead:
- Accept if comp is $400K+ total and the role has a clear path to EM within 12 months
- Negotiate a 6-month review with explicit EM promotion criteria
- Clarify whether the IC role includes team-building responsibilities
