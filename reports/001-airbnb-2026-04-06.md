### Team Context

The Workflow Orchestration team owns Airbnb's orchestration layer -- the system that coordinates, executes, and monitors complex data workflows. Airbnb created Apache Airflow internally in 2014. The JD references Maestro (likely their next-gen internal platform), Airflow, Dagster, Flyte, Prefect, and Kubeflow. This is a foundational infrastructure team touching every data consumer in the company.

### Responsibilities (from JD)

1. Lead and grow a high-performing team designing, implementing, and operating distributed orchestration infrastructure running tens of thousands of daily workflows.
2. Define long-term technical vision and roadmap for orchestration aligned to Airbnb's Data Infrastructure and Analytics platform strategy.
3. Drive adoption of best-in-class workflow paradigms and tooling across data engineering, reliability, and ML teams.
4. Partner with Data Platform, Compute, Storage, Analytics, and ML Infrastructure teams.
5. Coach and mentor engineers -- technical judgment, clarity of thinking, ownership.
6. Stay connected to architectural decisions, review designs, unblock technical challenges.
7. Ensure strong delivery discipline, operational monitoring, and on-call readiness.
8. Collaborate with senior engineering leaders on multi-year strategies.
9. Recruit top engineering talent with focus on inclusive leadership and psychological safety.

### Required Qualifications (from JD)

1. 5+ years of engineering leadership experience managing and scaling distributed systems teams.
2. Strong technical background in distributed systems, workflow orchestration engines, scheduling systems, or data infrastructure tooling.
3. Previous working experience in large-scale orchestration frameworks (Apache Airflow, Dagster, Flyte, Prefect, Kubeflow, Maestro or similar).
4. Understanding of challenges around scaling, observability, and multi-tenancy.
5. Strong familiarity with software engineering principles (OOP, FP, design patterns, code quality).
6. Track record of partnering across engineering, product, and cross-functional teams to deliver reliable infrastructure at scale.
7. Excellent written and verbal communication skills -- synthesizing complex technical concepts for broad audiences.

**Narrative to deploy:**
> "I've been operating as a player-coach for 6 years -- architecting platforms, leading teams of 4-6 engineers, and driving cross-org adoption. **Key framing moves:**

3. Position Amazon tenure as "started at Amazon, understand big-tech operational rigor."
4. Frame Colorado payroll migration as proof of delivery discipline and stakeholder management.

**If down-leveled:**
Airbnb might offer a Staff Engineer (IC) role on the same team instead. This is acceptable if comp is comparable. The Staff SWE, Workflow Orchestration role (position 6424477) is also open -- could be a fallback. At Airbnb, Staff IC and M1 EM are at the same level band.
