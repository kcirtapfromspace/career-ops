### About the Team
Core builds the technical foundation behind Google's flagship products. The Information Retrieval Embeddings Platform is a hosted solution for computing and serving embeddings models at scale. It integrates multiple systems: inference service, data processing pipeline, experimentation framework, and ScaM, orchestrating data flow between systems and providing monitoring capabilities.

### Responsibilities
1. Collaborate with client and partner teams to define and achieve product requirements for launches and ongoing enhancements
2. Partner with infrastructure teams to ensure cost-effectiveness, low latency, and development of essential features
3. Design, prototype, and orchestrate innovative embeddings-related developer journeys
4. Integrate IR embeddings technology within client-specific GenAI/LLM applications
5. Lead development of indexing and serving infrastructure, performance optimization, and custom solutions

### Minimum Qualifications
- Experience building large-scale infrastructure, distributed systems, or compute technologies
- Experience with Machine Learning Infrastructure
- Experience working with C++, Flume, and SQL

### Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with generative AI, machine learning, and vector databases
- Familiarity with Google internal infrastructure (Borg, Boq, Spanner, Flume)
