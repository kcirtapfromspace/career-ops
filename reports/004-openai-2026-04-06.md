### OpenAI EM Bar

OpenAI EMs are expected to:
1. Manage teams of senior/staff-level engineers who are among the best in the industry
2. Set technical direction while delegating execution
3. Navigate a hyper-growth organization (500 -> 3000+ employees in 2 years)
4. Operate in an environment where ICs may be stronger technically than the manager

Patrick's 90% IC / 10% management split at Ad Hoc is the opposite of what OpenAI needs. This is the fundamental positioning challenge.

### Strategy: "Technical Leader Transitioning to Full EM"

**Positioning (honest framing):**
- "I've spent 14 years as a technical builder, including 3 years leading data infrastructure teams delivering mission-critical systems for 100M+ users. I'm now seeking a dedicated EM role because I've realized my highest-leverage contribution is enabling engineers to do their best work, not writing the code myself."
- Lead with the cross-team influence: "At VA.gov, I didn't just lead a team of 4 -- I established observability standards adopted by 40+ engineering teams. That's the kind of organizational impact I want to scale."
- Colorado payroll: "I owned the end-to-end migration of 100K+ employee records from mainframe to cloud. That required managing stakeholders across state government, setting technical direction, and ensuring zero-downtime delivery."

### If Down-leveled

OpenAI might offer a Senior EM or a Staff+ IC role instead. Given the comp structure:
- Staff IC at OpenAI: $250K-$385K base + equity = potentially $600K-$1M+ TC
- This would still significantly exceed Patrick's $200K-$230K target
- Accept if: scope is interesting, path to EM is clear, comp is right
- Negotiate: 6-month review for EM promotion with defined criteria
