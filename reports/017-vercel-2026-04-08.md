## C) Level and Strategy

### Level Assessment

- **JD level:** Engineering Manager (3+ yr EM, 5+ yr IC). This is a standard EM, not Senior EM.
- **Patrick's natural level for this archetype:** IC Staff / borderline EM. Strong technical depth, emerging management track.
- **Gap:** The EM experience is borderline. 3 years is the minimum the JD states, and Patrick has ~3 years of informal leadership. Vercel may view this as sufficient given strong technical chops, or may flag it.

### Strategy: "Sell EM without inflating"

1. **Lead with the Watchtower story:** "I built VA.gov's observability platform -- I led a team of 4, drove architecture, coordinated across 40+ product teams, and delivered the system that became the VA's standard. That's management in high-stakes environments."
2. **Amazon interim PM:** "I managed a $5M building expansion at Amazon, launching a fulfillment center on schedule. I've managed people, budgets, and timelines."
3. **Frame the transition:** "I've deliberately moved from IC to leadership. I chose Staff Architect roles where I could lead teams while staying technical -- now I want the title to match the work I've been doing."
4. **Technical credibility as differentiator:** "Most EM candidates at this level are 2-3 years removed from code. I'm still shipping Rust and building production systems. For an AI Gateway team, that technical depth means I can unblock my engineers on architecture, not just process."

### If downleveled

- Accept Senior Engineer if comp is fair ($200K+ base, strong equity)
- Negotiate 6-month review with explicit EM promotion criteria
- Use the IC seat to learn Vercel's codebase and build internal credibility
