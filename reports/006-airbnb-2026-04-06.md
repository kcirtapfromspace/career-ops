## C) Level & Strategy

### Level Assessment

| Dimension | JD Level | Candidate Level | Delta |
|-----------|----------|----------------|-------|
| **Title** | Senior Staff Data Engineer (L7) | Staff Data Architect (current title) | -1 level |
| **YoE** | 12+ years | ~15 years (2010–2025) | Match |
| **Scope** | Cross-org data architecture for Users/Listings/Marketplaces | Cross-agency data architecture (VA, Colorado, HHS) | Comparable scope, different domain |
| **Tech depth** | Petabyte Spark/Java/Scala | Kafka/dbt/DuckDB/Argo/Rust/Go | Adjacent but different stack |
| **Organizational influence** | Set data engineering standards across Airbnb | Set observability standards across 40+ VA.gov teams | Comparable pattern, smaller org |

**Assessment:** Patrick's career arc and scope of influence are at Staff level (L6 at Airbnb). L7 (Senior Staff) is a stretch. He has the years, the cross-team influence, and the architectural thinking — but Airbnb L7 typically means defining company-wide data practices, mentoring Staff engineers, and driving multi-quarter strategic initiatives at a company with 6,000+ employees. His cross-agency government work is the closest analog, but it lacks the tight product feedback loops Airbnb expects.

### Strategy: "Sell Staff without overselling"

1. **Lead with architectural scope, not just execution.** "Designed data architecture spanning 40+ product teams and 18M+ end users" vs. "built ETL pipelines."
2. **Emphasize the reconciliation layer** (Colorado) — this is exactly the kind of deep data modeling work Airbnb needs for entity models. Mapping 30+ years of schema drift to modern data contracts = designing entity models for optimal storage.
3. **Highlight the Data Mesh pattern** — domain-specific data products with governance = exactly what Airbnb's Marketplaces DNA team does across Users/Listings/Pricing.
4. **Amazon experience as origin story** — "I started my career at Amazon fulfillment, understanding how marketplace operations work at scale. I've carried that product intuition into every data platform I've built since."

### If downleveled to L6 (Staff)

- Accept if total comp is $500K+ (still 2x+ target range).
- Negotiate a 6-month performance review with explicit L7 promotion criteria.
- Ask: "What does the path from Staff to Senior Staff look like on this team? What would I need to demonstrate in the first year?"
